Sudan Gurung

The Architect of Dissent & The "Civilian Force"

Executive Summary

Sudan Gurung represents a paradigm shift in Nepali politics—the collapse of traditional party hegemony (Congress, UML, Maoist) and the rise of network-centric, youth-led civic movements. Originally a nightlife entrepreneur, Gurung leveraged the credibility of his organization, Hami Nepal, during the 2015 Earthquake and COVID-19 pandemic to construct a formidable political vehicle.

By 2025, Gurung positioned himself as the vanguard of the "Gen Z Revolution," utilizing digital infrastructure to bypass established hierarchies. This report analyzes his trajectory, the weaponization of civil society, and the geopolitical complexities surrounding his ascent ahead of the 2026 General Elections.
